    What is “writing voice”?

    It's how your words sound. When we write a LinkedIn post, X thread, caption, carousel or article from your video, we follow your voice, so it reads like you wrote it and not like generic AI. It doesn't change how your videos look or are cut (that's “Styles”, under Videos).

    Teach us your voice

    Paste 2 or 3 posts you've written and liked. We pick up your sentence length, favourite words, how you open, and how you use emoji.
