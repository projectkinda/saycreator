At our customer day last month, a few people said things that stuck with us.

Maria Lopez, who runs support at a mid-sized logistics firm, said: "We stopped measuring how fast we close tickets and started measuring how many people never have to write to us again."

Daniel Okafor, an engineer who joined from a bank, told the room: "The best documentation I ever read was written by someone who had just been confused by the thing they were describing."

Priya Raman, who founded a two-person design studio, put it simply. "Small teams do not need more process. They need fewer surprises."

The host closed the day by saying that most of the improvements people described cost nothing: they were changes in what the team paid attention to.
